# HealMate

### Your space for mental wellness.

**[Explore the live app](https://healmate-app.vercel.app/)**

HealMate is an AI-powered mental wellness web application that brings mood journaling, personalized exercises, and online counselling into one platform. It helps users reflect on their emotions, explore everyday wellness practices, and connect with mental wellness consultants.

## Key Features

- **Mood journal:** Record daily moods with emojis and optional notes, then review previous entries.
- **Guided exercises:** Explore wellness activities for grounding, reflection, and managing everyday stress, with progress tracking.
- **Personalized recommendations:** Use onboarding responses and mood history to receive AI-assisted exercise and consultant recommendations.
- **Consultant booking:** Browse approved consultants, check their availability, and schedule appointments.
- **Online counselling:** Join scheduled video sessions through Jitsi Meet.
- **Payments and subscriptions:** Support session payments and tiered subscription plans through Stripe.

## User Roles

| Role | Main capabilities |
| --- | --- |
| User | Track moods, follow exercises, book sessions, and manage a subscription. |
| Consultant | Manage availability, view bookings, and conduct online sessions. |
| Admin | Approve consultants and manage users, bookings, safety alerts, and blog content. |

## Technology Stack

**Next.js** · **Laravel REST API** · **PostgreSQL** · **Stripe** · **Jitsi Meet**  
Frontend deployed on **Vercel**.
